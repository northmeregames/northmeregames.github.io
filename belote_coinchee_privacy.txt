# Privacy Policy — Belote Coinchée

This Privacy Policy explains how Belote Coinchée handles your information.

## Personal Information

You do not need to create an account or provide any personal information to use the application.

## Game Statistics

The application records game statistics to display your progress and performance.

These statistics are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to us or shared with third parties.

## Advertising

The application does not display advertisements.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your game statistics remain on your device until you uninstall the application or clear its data.

Uninstalling the application deletes locally stored statistics, subject to your device's backup and restoration settings.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published in the updated policy.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
